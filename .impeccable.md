## Design Context

### Users
VibePomodoro is for people doing deep work, study, or creative tasks who want a calm Pomodoro companion instead of a gamified productivity dashboard. They rely on trustworthy timekeeping before, during, and after focus sessions, including when the app moves through background and foreground states.

### Brand Personality
The product should feel calm, gentle, restrained, trustworthy, and quietly human. It should breathe, but never interrupt. It should feel like a steady focus companion rather than a cold instrument panel.

### Aesthetic Direction
The interface should stay within a refined Liquid Glass direction with subtle warmth and soft atmosphere. Prefer system-native glass semantics over fake blur-heavy treatments. Visual language should feel premium, quiet, and lightly organic, with warm amber, mist, and jade accents used sparingly.

### Design Principles
1. Precision is the foundation: timer truth and interface feedback must feel reliable at all times.
2. Calm over stimulation: avoid aggressive hierarchy, gamification, and dashboard-like noise.
3. Soft presence: motion, color, and glass effects should add companionship without pulling focus.
4. Native elegance: preserve system-aligned glass behavior and restrained detailing over decorative excess.
5. Structural polish without visual drift: refactors and UI cleanup should improve clarity and maintainability without changing the approved Focus experience.
